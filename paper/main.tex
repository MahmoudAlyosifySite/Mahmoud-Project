\section{Learning what never changes}
Let $z_t$ be the latent of frame $t$ and $u_t = (z_t,\; z_t - z_{t-1})$. Let $\phi(u)$ collect all
monomials of $u$ up to degree two. An invariant is $I(u) = w^\top (\phi(u) - \bar\phi)$ with $w$
solving
\begin{equation}
  \min_w \; \mathbb{E}\big[\big(w^\top(\phi(u_{t_2}) - \phi(u_{t_1}))\big)^2\big]
  \quad \text{s.t.} \quad \mathrm{Var}\big[w^\top \phi(u)\big] = 1,
\end{equation}
where $t_1, t_2$ are two event-free steps of the same clip, a long lag apart, and the variance is
over all clips. This is a generalised eigenproblem; its smallest eigenvalues give the invariants.
