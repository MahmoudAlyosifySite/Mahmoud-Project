\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
\item Compute-matched 2x2 design separates the use of labels from the choice of images
\item Labels inside a contrastive loss speed up pretraining but saturate early
\item The evaluation protocol and the split of the labels change which encoder wins
\item Label-using encoders tighten classes far more on labeled than on other images
\item Given enough pretraining, scarce labels are best spent after it, not in the loss
\end{itemize}
